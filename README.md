Showcase job simulations and talent profiles

Aganze NetworkTalent Platform is a skills‑first talent discovery system that helps job seekers showcase the job simulations, virtual internships, and hands‑on training programs they’ve completed in a way that is easy for employers to evaluate.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🎓 For Candidates
- Create a public profile
- Upload job simulations, certificates, and skills gained
- Build a portfolio employers can browse
- Get discovered based on practical experience

### 🏢 For Employers
- Browse talent by skills, industry, or simulation type
- View candidate portfolios and completed simulations
- Contact candidates directly
- Identify job‑ready talent faster

---

## 🧭 How It Works

1. **Create your profile**  
   Add your background, skills, and career goals.

2. **Upload job simulations**  
   Showcase certificates, summaries, and skills gained.

3. **Get discovered**  
   Employers browse talent and reach out directly.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To bridge the gap between proven skills and real hiring opportunities by giving job seekers visibility and giving companies access to motivated, job‑ready talent.

---

## 💡 Why This Platform Exists

Thousands of people complete job simulations everyday cybersecurity labs, data challenges, business case studies, virtual internships but most of this work is never seen by employers.

Aganze NetworkTalent Platform changes that by making practical experience visible, searchable, and valued.

Thank you for your interest in contributing!  
This project aims to help job seekers showcase job simulations and help employers discover talent based on proven skills.

## 🧭 Getting Started

1. Fork the repository  
2. Clone your fork  
3. Create a new branch  
4. Make your changes  
5. Commit with clear messages  
6. Open a Pull Request

## 🛠️ Development Guidelines

### Code Style
- Use clean, readable HTML, CSS, and JavaScript  
- Keep components modular  
- Avoid inline styles when possible  
- Comment complex logic clearly
